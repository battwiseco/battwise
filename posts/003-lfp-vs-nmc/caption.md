## Caption

LFP or NMC? The two battery types inside most EVs, power stations and home batteries, compared in 7 slides. 🔋

NMC: more energy per kg, so more range and less weight.
LFP: more cycles, a bigger thermal safety margin, and no nickel or cobalt.

Save this for the next time you shop for an EV or a power station.

Sources are on the last slide.
