How it works

LFP vs NMC in 7 slides

The two battery types inside most EVs, power stations and home batteries. Here's how to tell which one you want.

Same family, different recipe

Both are lithium-ion. The difference is the cathode.

LFP

Lithium iron phosphate. No nickel, no cobalt.

NMC

Lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxide.

The cathode chemistry decides energy, lifespan, safety margin and cost.

Round 1 · Energy

NMC packs more energy per kilogram

More energy per kg means more range or a lighter device.

Living with it

The trade-offs nobody puts on the box

MaterialsLFP uses no nickel or cobalt, so it's usually cheaper
Charging to 100%LFP handles it better. Check your manual; many LFP cars recommend it.
Deep coldNMC usually copes better
Weight and spaceNMC wins, so it's used where every kg matters
The verdict

Long life and big margins: LFP. Max range, light weight: NMC.
