How many charges is your battery built for? Apple says 500 to 1,000 full cycles. 🔋

One cycle = using 100% of the battery in total, even across several charges. 60% today + 40% tomorrow = 1 cycle.

Apple's ratings (to 80% capacity, under ideal conditions):
iPhone 14 and older: 500 cycles
iPhone 15: 1,000 cycles
iPad, MacBook, Apple Watch: 1,000 cycles

At one cycle a day, 1,000 cycles is under 3 years. Keep it cool and skip long stays at 100% to get the most out of every one.
