## Caption

Should you drain your phone to 0% before charging? Myth. 🔋

The "memory effect" was a problem for old nickel-based batteries. Lithium-ion has no memory, and smaller discharges actually give it more cycles.

So top up whenever it suits you. Short, frequent charges are easier on the battery than running it flat.

Follow for more battery myths, busted.
