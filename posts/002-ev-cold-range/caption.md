## Caption

Winter can cut your EV's range by up to 41%. ❄️

In AAA's lab test of five EVs at −7°C (20°F), range dropped 12% with the heater off and 41% with it on. Most of the loss is heating, not the battery itself.

The fix: pre-heat the car while it's still plugged in, so the warm-up runs on grid power instead of your range.

Send this to an EV driver before the first cold snap.

Source: AAA, 2019 (5 EVs, dynamometer, climate-controlled lab).
