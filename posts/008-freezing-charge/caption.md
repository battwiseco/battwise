## Caption

Is it fine to charge your e-bike in a freezing garage? Myth. ❄️

Below 0°C (32°F), consumer lithium-ion batteries shouldn't be charged. Lithium plates onto the anode as metal. It looks like it's charging normally, but the damage is permanent and hurts both capacity and safety.

Riding in the cold is fine. Charging cold is the problem. Bring the battery inside, let it warm up, then charge. Best range: 10–30°C (50–86°F). Your manual's limits come first.

Send this to someone who rides all winter.

Source: Battery University, BU-410.
