## Caption

What actually happens inside your battery when you plug it in? 🔌

Charging pushes lithium ions from the cathode (+) to the anode (−), through the electrolyte and a separator thinner than a hair. Using your phone sends them back, and the electrons flowing through your device on the way are the power you use.

Every trip traps a tiny bit of lithium, which is why capacity fades over time. Heat and charging in freezing cold make it worse.
