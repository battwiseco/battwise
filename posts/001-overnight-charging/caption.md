## Caption

Does charging overnight ruin your phone battery? Mostly myth. 🔋

Your phone stops charging at 100%, so it can't "overcharge." The real wear on battery health comes from sitting at full charge while it's warm, night after night.

The fix takes 10 seconds: turn on Optimized or Adaptive Charging (the menu paths are on slide 7), and never charge under your pillow.

Save this for later, and send it to the friend who unplugs at 3 a.m.

Sources are on the last slide.

## Alt text (per slide)

1. Title slide: "Does charging overnight ruin your battery?" A battery icon at 100% next to a crescent moon.
2. "Mostly myth" stamp. Your phone can't overcharge: when the battery is full, the phone's charging circuit stops drawing current from the charger.
3. Line chart of charge level over time: it rises fast, slows down near full, then holds at 100% with only small top-ups.
4. The real catch: lithium-ion ages faster when it sits at a high charge, and faster still when it's warm.
5. Bar chart of capacity left after one year of storage. At 25°C: 96% when kept at 40% charge vs 80% at 100%. At 40°C: 85% vs 65%. Data: Battery University BU-808.
6. Three heat tips: don't charge under a pillow, remove the case if it gets hot, keep it out of the sun. Apple's ideal range is 16–22°C; avoid above 35°C.
7. How to turn on smart charging. iPhone 15+: Settings > Battery > Charging. iPhone 14 and older: Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging. Pixel: Settings > Battery > Battery health > Charging optimization. Samsung: look for Protect battery.
8. Verdict: charge overnight, keep it cool, turn on smart charging. Sources listed.
