Myth or fact?

Does charging overnight ruin your battery?

A battery engineer's answer, in 8 slides.

MOSTLY MYTH

Your phone can't overcharge.

When the battery is full, the phone's charging circuit stops drawing current from the charger, so no charging current flows. Leaving it plugged in won't "overfill" it.

But there is a small catch. Keep swiping.

How it actually charges

Fast, then slow, then stop.

Once it reaches 100%, the phone only tops up a little now and then.

The real catch

It's not the charging. It's full + hot.

Lithium-ion ages faster when it sits at a high charge for hours, and faster still when it's warm.

One night won't hurt it much, but hundreds of nights add up.

Heat is the bigger enemy

Keep it cool while it charges.

  • 1Don't charge it under a pillow or blanket.
  • 2If it gets hot on the charger, take it out of its case.
  • 3Keep it out of the sun and off hot surfaces.

Apple: the ideal room temperature is 16–22°C (62–72°F). Avoid anything above 35°C (95°F).

The 10-second fix

Turn on smart charging.

Your phone learns your routine, holds at about 80% overnight, and fills to 100% just before you wake up.

  • iPhone 15+
    Settings › Battery › Charging
  • iPhone 14 and older
    Settings › Battery › Battery Health & Charging
  • Pixel
    Settings › Battery › Battery health › Charging optimization
  • Samsung & others
    Battery settings › look for "Protect battery" or a charge limit
The verdict

Charge overnight. Keep it cool. Turn on smart charging.
