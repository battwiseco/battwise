Does charging overnight ruin your phone battery? Mostly myth. 🔋

Your phone stops charging at 100%, so it can't "overcharge." The real wear on battery health comes from sitting at full charge while it's warm, night after night.

The fix takes 10 seconds: turn on Optimized or Adaptive Charging (the menu paths are on slide 7), and never charge under your pillow.
