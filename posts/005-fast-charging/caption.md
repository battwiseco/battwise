## Caption

Does fast charging ruin your battery? Mostly myth. ⚡

A battery takes charge fastest when it's low, and your phone slows the current down as it fills. The wear that does happen is in the last stretch to 100%, and especially when the battery is hot or cold.

So use the fast charger your device supports. Just keep it cool while it charges, and let a freezing-cold device warm up first.

Send this to the friend who still refuses to use their fast charger.
